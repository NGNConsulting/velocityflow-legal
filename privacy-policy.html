VelocityFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

VelocityFlow ("the app") is a personal mortgage-payoff planning and tracking tool published by NGN Consulting Corp. This policy explains what the app does, and does not, do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. VelocityFlow does not have user accounts, does not require sign-up, and does not connect to your bank or any financial institution. Every number you enter — your mortgage balance, HELOC details, income, and bills — is stored only in a local database on your own phone. NGN Consulting Corp cannot see, access, or receive any of this information, because it is never transmitted anywhere.

Subscriptions and payment

VelocityFlow offers optional paid subscriptions and a one-time purchase, processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — that is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. VelocityFlow only stores, locally on your device, whether you currently have an active subscription.

Third parties

The only third-party service involved in using VelocityFlow is Google Play Billing, for processing optional purchases. VelocityFlow contains no advertising, no analytics software, and no crash-reporting service as of this version.

Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is stored anywhere except your own device, uninstalling VelocityFlow permanently deletes all of your data. There is no server-side copy to separately request deletion of.

Children's privacy

VelocityFlow is not directed at children and is not designed for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case the app collects no information from anyone at all, as described above.

Not financial advice

VelocityFlow is for planning and educational use only. It is not a lender, bank, broker, or financial advisor, and makes no promises about any outcome. It does not provide financial, investment, or tax advice, and every projection it shows is an estimate calculated from the numbers you enter — not a guarantee of any outcome.
